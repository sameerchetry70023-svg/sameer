How the system works

CapturePhoto from the camera or an uploaded file.
Pre-processResize to 224 × 224, normalise colour, remove noise.
ClassifyA CNN (for example MobileNetV2 trained on TrashNet or TACO) scores five waste classes.
AdviseShow the bin, disposal steps and the CO₂ saved, then log the scan.
